E-Learning Mobile App • Prepanova

CourSpace

CourSpace (Course Space) is an Algerian e-learning mobile application developed as my final graduation project completing the 2-year preparatory cycle (Projet de Fin d'Études de Prépa — Prepanova) at ENSTA.

Conceived to eliminate fragmented searches across disparate platforms, CourSpace operates as an all-in-one learning map for Algerian students. It centralizes top online and in-person courses, curated programming tracks, and interactive roadmaps into a unified, user-friendly mobile experience.

Project Highlights

  • 2-Year Prepa Culmination: Capstone project marking the completion of the 2-year intensive preparatory cycle.
  • Algeria's Learning Map: Centralized hub cataloging vetted educational courses and opportunities nationwide.
  • Mobile-First Design: Clean, intuitive Android UI tailored for effortless self-paced learning and course tracking.
